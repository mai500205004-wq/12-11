## Brand & Style

This design system expresses quiet royal power, sovereign heritage, and bespoke craftsmanship. Tailored for ultra-high-net-worth patrons, royal archives, private high-jewelry ateliers, and bespoke luxury houses, the experience mirrors the atmosphere of a nocturnal private salon.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic
- **Architectural Classical-Modernism:** Grounded in marble masonry, grand axial symmetry, and generous spatial discipline.
- **Atmospheric Chandelier Glow:** Controlled, diffuse warm amber reflections cutting through deep obsidian velvet.
- **Restrained Decadence:** Opulence conveyed through precise hairline gold work, generous negative space, and curated typographic contrast rather than loud decorative clutter.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration from nightfall in sovereign palace courtyards, aged parchment, and brushed bullion gold.

### Color Roles & Atmosphere
- **Canvas / Floor:** Obsidian Velvet (`#0B0B0D`) forms the absolute background depth, supported by warm charcoal tiers (`#111114`).
- **Surfaces & Chambers:** Dark slate basalt with deep warm undertones (`#17171B` for baseline elevation, `#1E1E24` for active surface panels).
- **Metals & Accents:**
  - **Primary Gold:** Brushed Champagne Gold (`#D4AF37`) for critical accents, focal call-to-actions, and structural highlights.
  - **Secondary Radiance:** Soft Champagne Glow (`#E5C365`) for hover states and luminous text accents.
  - **Muted Amber:** Royal Patina (`#C59B27`) for pressed states, badges, and regal filigree lines.
  - **Antique Crest:** Radiant Ivory Gold (`#F3E5AB`) for high-contrast micro-details and emblem highlights.
- **Typographic Palette:**
  - **Ivory Silk (`#F7F4EE`):** Primary text and display titles.
  - **Muted Cashmere (`#B8B3A8`):** Body narratives, subheadings, and secondary metadata.
  - **Chamber Shadow (`#78746D`):** Tertiary timestamps, inactive states, and structural hints.
- **Atmospheric Glow & Borders:**
  - Hairline gold strokes utilize `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.22)`.
  - Ambient chandelier wash uses radial drop falls of `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.12)`.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances majestic classical display serifs with contemporary, pristine geometric body typography and precise archival monospace tokens.

- **Editorial Headings (`Playfair Display`):** Carries authoritative presence, high optical stroke contrast, and poetic grace. Headings should be composed with ample breathing room and comfortable, generous line heights.
- **Refined Body Text (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Selected for its polished clarity and balanced proportions. Letter tracking is slightly loosened to maintain an unhurried, comfortable reading rhythm against deep dark backgrounds.
- **Curatorial Indexing (`Space Mono`):** Applied strictly to provenance codes, serial numbers, date seals, and regal classification tags. Rendered in all-caps with generous letter-spacing (`0.14em` to `0.18em`).

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow strict structural symmetry evocative of royal gallery architecture and grand saloons.

### Layout Rhythm & Grids
- **Desktop (12 Columns):** Fixed max-width container (`1440px`), 4rem outer canvas margins, and 2rem gutters. Centered axial alignment anchors major milestones, showcase portfolios, and title treatments.
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** Fluid container with 2rem margins and 1.5rem gutters. Dual-pane catalog spreads reflow into single columns with preserved margin breathing space.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** Fluid layout with 1.25rem margins and 1rem gutters. Display headings scale down to preserve structural lines without unintended word breaks.

### Spacing Philosophy
- White space is treated as architectural space. Dense arrangements are rejected in favor of measured intervals (`space-lg` and `space-xl`) between narrative passages and inventory showcases.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained through deep tonal surfaces, subtle gold-rimmed outlines, and atmospheric amber dissipation.

### Elevation Hierarchy
1. **Basement / Foundation (`#0B0B0D`):** Zero elevation. Deep black obsidian void serving as the base gallery floor.
2. **Atelier Level 1 (`#111114`):** Base structural panels and background sidebars. Layered with a 1px boundary line in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`.
3. **Chamber Level 2 (`#17171B`):** Interactive cards, curatorial cases, and dialog backdrops. Finished with a 1px perimeter border of `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.18)` and an ultra-diffuse ambient drop shadow: `0 16px 36px -10px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7), 0 0 24px -4px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.06)`.
4. **Sovereign Overlay Level 3 (`#1E1E24`):** Menus, command drawers, and inspection sheets. Backed with a frosted glass treatment (`backdrop-filter: blur(24px) saturate(140%)`) and an inner specular rim highlight: `inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(243, 229, 171, 0.25)`.
5. **Chandelier Bloom:** Hero sections and key interactive focal points feature a soft radial gradient layer (`radial-gradient(ellipse at 50% 0%, rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.12) 0%, rgba(11, 11, 13, 0) 70%)`).

## Shapes

The shape system references hand-cut stone masonry and architectural architraves. 

- **Corner Radii:** Strictly soft-edged (`roundedness: 1`), keeping elements crisp, structured, and stately. Standard interactive components receive `0.25rem` (4px), cards and surfaces use `0.5rem` (8px), and hero containers take `0.75rem` (12px).
- **Proportions:** Pill shapes and circular components are restricted entirely to royal wax-seal badges and monogram crests. Rectangles and framed squares govern all functional controls and data layouts.
- **Hairline Precision:** Internal dividers and framing edges maintain an unvarying `1px` structural stroke.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Sovereign Primary:** Background in Brushed Champagne Gold (`#D4AF37`), text in Obsidian Velvet (`#0B0B0D`) set in `Plus Jakarta Sans` semi-bold with wide tracking. Outer edge finished with a hairline rim of Antique Gold (`#F3E5AB`). On hover, transitions to Soft Champagne Glow (`#E5C365`) accompanied by an ambient gold aura (`box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35)`).
- **Atelier Secondary:** Transparent background, 1px solid border in `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35)`, text in Warm Alabaster (`#F7F4EE`). On hover, background fills with `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08)` and border brightens to `#D4AF37`.
- **Text Link / Quiet Action:** Ivory Silk text with a discrete gold underline offset by `6px`, transitioning into a full gold illumination on hover.

### Cards & Curatorial Cases
- Crafted on `#17171B` with subtle corner radii (8px). 
- Bounded by a 1px gradient stroke running from `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35)` at the top edge to `rgba(43, 42, 51, 0.4)` at the bottom edge.
- Inner content padding uses `space-lg` (2.25rem) to ensure imagery and provenance narratives breathe without confinement.

### Inputs & Selectors
- Background is `#111114` with a 1px border of `#2B2A33`.
- Placeholder text in `#78746D`, and active input text in Ivory Silk (`#F7F4EE`).
- Focused state transitions the border to Brushed Champagne Gold (`#D4AF37`) with an inner top hairline illumination and zero harsh outer glow.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Custom square frame (for checkboxes) or diamond-oriented glyph (for radios) in 1px gold wireframe (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.4)`).
- Checked state fills the interior with deep charcoal (`#17171B`) and stamps a solid Champagne Gold crest or chevron mark (`#D4AF37`).

### Chips, Seals & Badges
- **Heritage Badges:** Background in `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.1)`, 1px border in `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25)`, typography set in `Space Mono` (`label-sm`) in Antique Gold (`#F3E5AB`).
- **Monogram Seal:** Circular insignia (32px to 48px) framed by concentric 1px gold rings with a centralized serif ligature mark.

### Lists & Archival Catalogs
- Dividers are fine hairlines (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`).
- List items feature generous vertical padding (`space-md`), subtle gold hover indicator markers along the left margin, and balanced dual-column layout mapping labels to archival metadata.
