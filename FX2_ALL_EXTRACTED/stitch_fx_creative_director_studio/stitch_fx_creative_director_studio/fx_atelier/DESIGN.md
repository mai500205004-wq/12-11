## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the discipline of an arthouse film atelier, orchestrating a high-contrast collision between haute-couture editorial typography and the unsparing precision of a cinematographic editing suite. Built for visionary directors, master copywriters, and avant-garde creative directors, the interface discards mundane SaaS conventions—banishing generic rounded cards, buoyant dropshadows, and neon gradient tropes.

Instead, the aesthetic commands respect through architectural severity: razor-sharp corners, pitch obsidian planes, hairline grid partitions, and warm incandescent tungsten illumination. It feels tactile yet ethereal, echoing a 35mm negative light table, a director's leather-bound production ledger, and an archival literary review. Interaction is deliberate, cerebral, and authoritative, framing artificial intelligence not as a novelty assistant, but as an invisible, razor-sharp creative collaborator.

## Colors

The palette is tuned around the physics of light within a darkroom, contrasting deep obsidian shades against warm tungsten filament incandescence and archival parchment.

- **Obsidian Darkroom Surfaces**:
  - `Canvas / Core`: `#0B0C10` (Absolute void black-slate)
  - `Surface / Atelier`: `#12131A` (Layer 1 containment, raw negative base)
  - `Surface / Cell`: `#191B24` (Elevated modules, monitor wells, metadata slabs)
- **Film Luminescence (Accents)**:
  - `Primary / Tungsten Amber`: `#FFB347` (Key action points, focal cues, active state indicators)
  - `Secondary / Cine Flame`: `#E06D3B` (Dramatic alerts, critical takes, director flags)
  - `Tertiary / Raw Brass`: `#D4AF37` (Archive tags, approved cuts, golden-ratio guidelines)
- **Editorial Parchment (Typography & Highlights)**:
  - `Text / Brilliant Script`: `#F6F3EE` (Headlines, primary literary passages)
  - `Text / Muted Vellum`: `#DDD7CD` (Secondary commentary, narrative body)
  - `Text / Slate Metadata`: `#75798A` (Timecodes, frame counters, optical data)
- **Structural Lines**:
  - `Border / Hairline Frame`: `rgba(246, 243, 238, 0.08)` (Subtle editorial alignment boundaries)
  - `Border / Active Optical`: `rgba(255, 179, 71, 0.45)` (Focus and selection borders)

## Typography

The typographic tension is the engine of the entire brand. It operates via the stark juxtaposition of extreme refinement against cold utility:

- **The Editorial Voice (`Bodoni Moda`)**: High-contrast, extreme serifs used for literary treatment, scene headlines, screenplay excerpts, and thematic manifestos. It communicates timeless authorship, luxury, and art-historical pedigree.
- **The Telemetry Slate (`JetBrains Mono`)**: Strict, technical monospacing that renders script supervisor notes, focal lengths, shutter angles, AI model parameters, and SMPTE timecodes (`01:14:22:18`).
- **Bilingual & Arabic Treatment**: For Arabic typography counterparting `Bodoni Moda`, integrate high-contrast traditional Amiri or modern high-contrast Naskh display cuts with sharp geometric tension. For Arabic metadata, maintain condensed, tabular monospace styling to retain the cinematographic slate aesthetic.
- **Typographic Rules**: Always set metadata labels in uppercase with deliberate letter-spacing (`0.08em` to `0.12em`). Do not bold editorial serifs unnecessarily; let the extreme vertical stroke weight contrast carry optical prominence.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout discards symmetrical card grids in favor of an asymmetrical, editorial light-table structure:

- **Grid Architecture**: A modular 12-column architectural grid defined by visible, muted hairline rules instead of floating margins. Surfaces dock flush against one another like film strips on a splicing bench.
- **Zonal Partitioning**:
  - *Left Pillar*: Fixed 280px monospaced project index, scene logs, and optical parameters.
  - *Center Core*: Fluid editorial canvas with expansive negative space for screenwriting treatments, dramatic storyboards, and AI generations.
  - *Right Pillar*: Telemetry panel for prompt dissection, chromatic tuning, and voice analysis.
- **Breakpoints**:
  - `Mobile` (< 768px): Single-column stack. Telemetry transforms into bottom-sheet overlays; editorial serif titles downscale to prevent awkward character hyphenation. Outer margin collapses to `1.25rem`.
  - `Tablet` (768px - 1024px): Dual-pane split (index collapses to collapsible glyph rail, canvas and telemetry remain).
  - `Desktop` (> 1024px): Full non-linear 3-pane atelier spread with wide `3.5rem` margins and `2.5rem` gutters.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through luminosity tiers and chromatic refraction rather than generic blur drop-shadows:

- **Surface Tiers**:
  - `Level 0 (Negative Void)`: `#0B0C10` background base.
  - `Level 1 (Splicing Table)`: `#12131A` framed with `1px solid rgba(246, 243, 238, 0.08)`.
  - `Level 2 (Active Cell / Slate)`: `#191B24` with high-precision hairline perimeter.
- **Tungsten Backlight Projection**: Modals, active states, and focal nodes project a soft amber bloom (`box-shadow: 0 0 40px -10px rgba(255, 179, 71, 0.15)`), mimicking light leaking around matte-box flags on set.
- **Optical Glass & Diffraction**: Overlays and status indicators utilize fine 1px prismatic refraction borders—subtle split offsets of red and cyan fringing (`1px 0 rgba(224, 109, 59, 0.4), -1px 0 rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.4)`) to evoke anamorphic lens flares.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary is unapologetically uncompromising:

- **Zero-Radius Mandate (`0px`)**: Every edge, panel, button, tag, and modal possesses an exact, sharp 90-degree corner. No roundedness (`border-radius: 0px`) is permitted anywhere within the design system.
- **Film Aperture Proportions**: Image containers, clip viewports, and script cards conform strictly to cinematic aspect ratios: `2.39:1` (Anamorphic), `1.85:1` (Theatrical Standard), `1.33:1` (Academy Ratio), or `1:1` (Contact Sheet).
- **Hairline Framing**: Structural containers employ crisp `1px` borders, creating a mechanical blueprint precision reminiscent of camera optical markers and production clapperboards.

## Components

### Buttons & Trigger Slates
- **Primary Action (Tungsten Slate)**: Solid `#FFB347` background with `#0B0C10` monospaced text. Zero radius. Hover shifts to raw amber warmth with a delicate `0 0 20px rgba(255, 179, 71, 0.3)` glow.
- **Secondary (Editorial Negative)**: Pitch `#12131A` background, `1px solid rgba(246, 243, 238, 0.15)` border, `#F6F3EE` label. On hover, the border flashes `#FFB347` with a discreet audio-click tactile cue.
- **Telemetry Ghost**: No background, monospaced text with `[ ]` bracketing indicators: `[ RUN_TAKE_04 ]`.

### Chips & Scene Metatags
- Compact rectangular blocks with `0px` radius.
- Background: `#191B24`; border: `1px solid rgba(246, 243, 238, 0.08)`.
- Text styled in `slate-mono` (`JetBrains Mono`, 10px, uppercase).
- Status markers utilize tiny square micro-indicators (1px pure amber or scarlet points) instead of rounded status pills.

### Lists & Ledger Logs
- Arranged like a director’s daily call sheet or negative ledger.
- Rows separated by razor-sharp `1px solid rgba(246, 243, 238, 0.05)` rules.
- Alternate metadata columns (Timecode, Focal Length, Scene, Sentiment) dynamically align across fixed tabular widths.
- Active row highlights through an incandescent left border line (`2px solid #FFB347`) and subtle `#191B24` fill shift.

### Checkboxes & Toggle Switches
- **Checkboxes**: Sharp 12x12px square outlines. Unchecked: `1px solid #75798A`. Checked: solid `#FFB347` fill with a sharp black crosshairs (+) glyph rather than a standard checkmark.
- **Toggles**: Segmented rectangular two-state rocker slates (`MANUAL` / `AUTONOMOUS`). Selected state receives solid `#FFB347` fill with `#0B0C10` bold mono type.

### Input Fields & Creative Prompters
- **Script Prompter**: Large-format, distraction-free textarea with a `#12131A` bed, no internal drop-shadow, and a subtle bottom border that turns to incandescent amber when focused.
- **Metadata Fields**: Preceded by persistent monospaced index labels (`LENS //`, `TONE //`, `ISO //`). Zero radius, background `#0B0C10`, text `#F6F3EE`.

### Master Cards & Scene Folios
- Constructed from flat `#12131A` layers bound by `1px solid rgba(246, 243, 238, 0.08)`.
- Cards feature top metadata bars displaying cue indices (`SCENE 44A - EXT. NIGHT`), separation rules, and prominent display serif titles.
- No soft shadows; stacking order is articulated exclusively by tonal contrast and razor-thin frame lines.

### Specialized Component: The Director's Slate
- A dual-display block fusing high-contrast Bodoni Moda creative titles with live running monospaced execution telemetry: SMPTE counter, aspect ratio locks, token consumption, and model temperance meter.
