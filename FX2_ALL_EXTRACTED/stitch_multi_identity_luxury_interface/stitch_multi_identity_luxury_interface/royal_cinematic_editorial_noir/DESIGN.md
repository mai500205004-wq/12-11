## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an elite, cinematic editorial noir aesthetic tailored for high-stakes creative intelligence and executive production suites. The visual language blends the gravitas of bespoke haute-couture print editorial with the tense, atmospheric depth of cinematic anamorphic film sets. 

The personality is authoritative, dramatic, and unflinchingly disciplined. It rejects sterile Silicon Valley SaaS tropes—discarding generic card corners, neon cyan blurs, and toy-like micro-interactions—in favor of pitch-black monolithic foundations, luminous tungsten-red filaments, and razor-sharp typographic hierarchy. 

Key experiential pillars:
- **Cinematic Chiaroscuro:** Deep pitch-charcoal backdrops punctuated by controlled ember and incandescent filament glows.
- **Editorial Authority:** Grand, high-contrast titles counterbalanced by disciplined technical monospaced metadata, providing clear narrative weight across Arabic and Latin scripts.
- **Bilingual Symmetry:** Parity across RTL (Arabic) and LTR (English) orientations, preserving spatial tension, narrative flow, and typographic balance.
- **Tactile Monoliths:** Heavy structural panels reminiscent of cinema master monitors, optical cutting benches, and archive leather folios.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in absolute optical density, driven by deep volcanic charcoals, pure light-absorbing voids, and incandescent warm embers.

### Base Canvases & Surfaces
- **Canvas Pitch (`#08090B`):** The primary subterranean background; captures absolute depth and suppresses ambient interface distraction.
- **Surface Noir (`#0E0E10`):** The primary work surface, framing scripts, timelines, and insight dossiers.
- **Surface Tactile Monolith (`#131417`):** Elevated structural blocks, drawers, and locked brief containers.
- **Surface Elevated Interactive (`#171719`):** Interactive inputs, active stage selectors, and hover elevations.
- **Surface Border Filament Base (`#1C1D21`):** Structural hairline divider tint; replaces standard system borders.

### Luminous Filaments & Embers
- **Filament Primary (`#F23827`):** High-tension red filament; active stage progression, focus rings, and primary action conduits.
- **Filament Ember (`#FF553E`):** Radiative edge tint; illuminates hot hover states and live processing beacons.
- **Filament Smolder (`#D73B00`):** Deep amber-red; utilized for persistent ambient radial gradients and low-stress status tracks.
- **Imperial Bronze / Gold (`#E5A93C`):** Restrained status indicator reserved strictly for archival unlocks, definitive strategic insights, and royal executive tags.

### Editorial Typography Tones
- **Text Haute Cream (`#E8E5E1`):** High-readability warm off-white for primary editorial headlines and active scripts.
- **Text Muted Parchment (`#C7C3BD`):** Secondary editorial descriptive copy, metadata headers, and field labels.
- **Text Ash (`#8E8A85`):** Monospaced technical parameters, timecodes, inactive switcher nodes, and structural notations.

## Typography

The typography establishes a tension between the dramatic geometric power of **Syne**, the classical literary nobility of **EB Garamond**, and the cinematic production rigor of **Space Mono**. 

### Typographic Roles & Script Integration
- **Display & Stage Markers (Syne):** Directs the 4 Core Stages and Identity realms. For Arabic script contexts, use **Cairo** (Black/Bold) to preserve the sharp, architectonic cuts and geometric discipline.
- **Editorial Narratives & Treatments (EB Garamond):** Renders creative briefs, narrative hypotheses, and cinematic screenplays. In Arabic contexts, pair with **Amiri** or **Tajawal** at medium weights to match the calligraphic refinement and royal editorial cadence.
- **System Telemetry & Metadata (Space Mono):** Used for locked status tags, identity codes, word counts, scene revisions, and coordinate indexes. Preserves mechanical precision.

### Typographic Discipline
- Never set running editorial copy in sans-serif. Narrative insights must retain literary gravitas.
- All monospaced labels (`label-caps`) are strictly uppercase in Latin, accompanied by tracked spacing (`0.14em`). In Arabic, they are formatted with subtle bracket accents `[ ]` or hairline framing instead of tracking.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is conceived as a wide cinematic canvas utilizing an asymmetrical 12-column editorial grid. It prioritizes vertical hierarchy, theatrical framing, and generous safe zones.

### The 4 Core Stages Navigation Grid
The core pipeline moves through 4 distinct creative stages:
1. **البريف Brief**
2. **الاستبصار Insight**
3. **المفهوم Concept**
4. **السيناريو Script**

This pipeline sits within an architectural header or persistent master ribbon. In desktop view, it spans all 12 columns with equidistant monolithic steps connected by an ultra-thin hairline conduit (`#1C1D21`). The active stage pulses with a 1px Filament Primary floor line. In RTL view, the timeline flows naturally from right to left with mirroring connection arrows.

### Identity Switcher Architecture
Anchored in the primary master utility bar, the four identity realms:
- **المواجهة Confrontation**
- **الاحتمالات Possibilities**
- **المسرح Theatre**
- **التحرير Editing**

These operate as a tactile radio-matrix. Switching realms subtly shifts the underlying filament ambient glow from focused red to burnt amber, signaling the current cognitive mode.

### Form Factor Adaptability
- **Desktop (1440px+):** 12-column fluid grid, 48px outer canvas margin. Wide screen ratios, dual-column editorial reading panes with lateral metadata columns.
- **Tablet (768px – 1439px):** 8-column layout, 32px margins. The 4 Stages condense into a compact numeric-stepper ribbon.
- **Mobile (Under 768px):** Single-column stacked canvas, 20px outer margin. The Identity Switcher condenses into an interactive executive dropdown with haptic feedback styling.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system rejects conventional dropped shadows and ambient white glows. Depth is created through optical occlusion, monolithic dark surfaces, and incandescent back-lit filaments.

### Elevation Levels

1. **The Absolute Abyss (Level 0 - `#08090B`):** The foundational substrate. Completely non-reflective.
2. **Monolithic Bedrock (Level 1 - `#0E0E10`):** Main stage views. Outlined by hairline borders (`1px solid #1C1D21`).
3. **Floating Dossier (Level 2 - `#131417`):** Elevated narrative blocks, insight panels, and stage cards. Features an internal top hairline highlight: `box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(232, 229, 225, 0.05)`.
4. **Active Filament Highlight (Level 3 - Interactive):** Focused elements leverage an ambient filament projection: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(242, 56, 39, 0.25), inset 0 0 1px 1px rgba(242, 56, 39, 0.4)`.

### The Locked Brief Box Specification
The Locked Brief Box is the core narrative artifact of the suite:
- **Surface:** Solid charcoal monolith (`#131417`).
- **Inner Corner Spotlight:** Radial spotlight anchored to the top-right corner (in RTL) or top-left corner (in LTR): `radial-gradient(circle at top right, rgba(242, 56, 39, 0.12) 0%, rgba(215, 59, 0, 0.04) 45%, transparent 70%)`.
- **Perimeter Filament Animation:** A continuous, understated filament trace traveling across 22% to 25% of the outer perimeter border, rendered via a moving linear conic-gradient mask using `#F23827` transitioning to zero opacity.
- **Status Monolith Stamp:** Secured with an amber-gold (`#E5A93C`) tamper-evident monospaced seal.

## Shapes

The shape philosophy is strictly **Sharp (0px radius)**. 

Curved, pill-shaped, or bubbly interfaces dilute cinematic gravitas. Precision-milled, razor-sharp edges evoke luxury archive cases, medium-format viewfinders, and high-end industrial film machinery.

- **Panels, Modals, Cards, and Inputs:** 0px border radius. Clean 90-degree miters.
- **Corner Notches (Architectural Variation):** High-priority dossiers and executive cards may employ an optical 45-degree corner notch (`clip-path: polygon(...)` 8px cut) on the top-right corner (LTR) or top-left corner (RTL), accentuating the tactile feel of physical production cue sheets.
- **Borders:** Maximum 1px thickness. Never use 2px or thicker borders; depth must be communicated through tonal difference and filament light emission, not heavy line weight.

## Components

### Buttons & Executive Triggers
- **Primary Filament Action:** Razor-sharp rectangle. Solid `#F23827` background, text in `#08090B` (bold Syne/Cairo). On hover, transitions to `#FF553E` with an outer 12px filament dispersion glow.
- **Secondary Dossier Trigger:** Translucent dark canvas (`#131417`), 1px hairline border (`#1C1D21`), text in `#E8E5E1`. On hover, the border turns `#F23827`, and the text shifts to `#FFFFFF`.
- **Ghost Metadata Trigger:** Space Mono uppercase text with an arrow glyph (`→` or `←` in RTL). No container. Underline appears as a luminous red filament on hover.

### The 4 Stages Navigator Component
- Displayed horizontally across the top section.
- Each stage includes a dual-index: Arabic title above, monospaced step counter below (e.g., `[ 01 // STAGE ]`).
- **Current Active Stage:** Underlined by an active filament bar with an ambient warm spill beneath it.
- **Completed Stages:** Rendered in `#C7C3BD` with a discrete check glyph in imperial bronze (`#E5A93C`).
- **Upcoming Stages:** Rendered in `#8E8A85` with zero glow.

### Identity Switcher Bar
- A monolithic switcher containing the four realms: المواجهة, الاحتمالات, المسرح, التحرير.
- Inactive items display in muted ash (`#8E8A85`) without a background.
- Active item snaps into a solid `#171719` enclosure with a 1px ember border (`#F23827`) and white editorial type.
- Smooth transition of the background canvas ambient gradient when the active identity realm changes.

### Locked Brief Box
- Monolithic container with a sharp 0px perimeter.
- Top-anchored spotlight fading from incandescent orange to pitch black.
- Perimeter filament running along 22-25% of the border circumference.
- Prominent monospaced classification header: `[ BRIEF STATUS: SEALED // NOIR ACCESS ONLY ]`.
- Content displayed in high-contrast editorial EB Garamond.

### Input Fields & Treatment Areas
- **Background:** `#0E0E10` sunken well.
- **Border:** 1px hairline `#1C1D21`.
- **Focus State:** 1px solid `#F23827`. No default browser outline. Subtle red glow inside the input well.
- **Typography:** Space Mono for code and prompt parameters; EB Garamond for long-form narrative treatments.

### Chips & Dossier Tags
- Boxy rectangular tags with 1px border.
- Regular tag: `#171719` fill, `#8E8A85` text.
- Executive/Gold tag: `#171719` fill, `#E5A93C` hairline border, `#E5A93C` text, indicating strategic unlocks or approved narrative paths.
