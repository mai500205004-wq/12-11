## Brand & Style
The design system manifests as an apex cognitive engine for creative directors, brand strategists, and advertising architects. It combines the rigorous discipline of an industrial terminal with the elevated, tactile gravitas of a high-fashion editorial archive. It discards mundane enterprise SaaS tropes in favor of an austere, high-focus studio environment: Cannes Lions Grand Prix war room meets high-performance neural workstation.

The aesthetic fuses **Architectural Brutalism** and **Tactile Precision Glass**:
- Razor-sharp linear dividers, laser-machined framing, and high-density telemetry.
- Monumental, expressive editorial typography contrasting against strict monospaced operational telemetry.
- Deep, atmospheric, low-reflectance carbon surfaces punctured by kinetic, high-velocity chromatic charges.

## Colors
The palette is rooted in an unyielding obsidian vacuum, illuminated selectively by incendiary optical heat.

- **Primary (`#F23827`)**: Hyper-radiant kinetic vermilion. Used exclusively for intentional creative vectors: execute triggers, active mutations, high-impact semantic nodes, and critical metabolic focus.
- **Secondary / Accent (`#FF6A2A`)**: Incandescent amber flare. Reserved for telemetry alerts, ideation friction, dynamic gradient shifts, and thermal activity signatures.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#08090B`)**: Deep obsidian void. The baseline canvas that absorbs visual clutter and isolates mental focus.
- **Surface Elevation (`#171719`)**: Matte titanium paneling. Provides discrete mechanical separation without lifting into luminous grey.
- **Subtle Borders (`#2A2A2E`)**: Ultra-fine mechanical laser lines. Micro-structures that establish rigid structural containment.
- **Text & Metadata**: Editorial ivory white (`#E8E5E1`) for pristine optical contrast; technical stone grey (`#8F8C87`) for secondary descriptive planes and telemetry.

## Typography
Typographic hierarchy is built on sharp friction: dramatic architectural headline statements colliding against utilitarian, neutral prose and monospaced telemetry.

- **Display & Headings (`Syne`)**: Avant-garde, sculptural, and unapologetically dominant. Used for campaign mandates, macro creative themes, and structural manifestos.
- **Body Text (`Inter`)**: Pure neutral clarity. Allows complex creative briefs, strategic scripts, and conceptual prose to breathe without typographic distraction.
- **Metadata & Telemetry (`JetBrains Mono`)**: Micro-precision data markers. Used strictly for creative genome indices, frame timestamps, model inference metrics, lens coordinates, and operational parameters. Always formatted with uppercase tracking where applicable (`label-caps`).

## Layout & Spacing
The layout operates as a modular, high-density optical workbench. It enforces an edge-to-edge structural framework bounded by sharp, relentless coordinate lines rather than floating islands.

- **Grid Architecture**: 12-column variable workbench grid on desktop with precision 1px dividing rules running down gutter axes. On desktop, gutters default to `1.25rem`, framed by a canvas perimeter inset of `2.5rem`.
- **Adaptation & Breakpoints**:
  - **Desktop (>= 1280px)**: 12-column multi-pane studio view (Idea Metabolism canvas, telemetric sidebar, script/treatment workspace).
  - **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 6-column modular flow; secondary lenses dock into swipeable drawer mechanics. Gutters compress to `1rem`.
  - **Mobile (< 768px)**: 2-column or single-stream focused cockpit; perimeter margins tighten to `1rem` and gutters to `0.75rem`.
- **Rhythm Rules**: Components leverage consistent internal density tokens (`space-xs` through `space-xl`) to preserve dense workspace utility without sacrificing scanning speed.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation in this system eschews standard drop shadows and soft skeuomorphism. Depth is produced purely through **surface tiering, structural laser borders, and optical kinetic glow**.

- **Level 0 (Canvas Void)**: `#08090B`. The baseline ground of all dynamic windows and ideation canvases.
- **Level 1 (Titanium Plates)**: `#171719` container fill with a hairline boundary: `1px solid #2A2A2E`. Zero box shadow. Flat, milled, industrial precision.
- **Level 2 (Active Focus & Overlays)**: Surface lightened to `#1E1E22`, flanked by an ultra-crisp kinetic border: `1px solid rgba(242, 56, 39, 0.4)`. Backdrops behind floating overlays employ a heavy background blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(24px) saturate(140%)`) tinted with 85% opacity `#08090B`.
- **Thermal Radiance (Micro-elevation)**: Active nodes, selected frames, and primed triggers cast a hyper-focused laser glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 20px -4px rgba(242, 56, 39, 0.45)`. Ambient spread is never muddy; it simulates a backlit optical aperture.

## Shapes
Geometry is engineered with an uncompromising architectural edge: **Sharp (`0px` border radius across all primary interactive surfaces)**.

- All containers, cards, viewports, buttons, and telemetry tags possess strict `0px` corners, maintaining the feeling of high-end optoelectronic equipment.
- Micro-exceptions are purely mechanical: status indicator pips and radio beacons remain circular geometries (`border-radius: 9999px`) to contrast against the rigid rectilinear matrix.
- Chamfered or cut-corner aesthetics (45-degree micro-notches) may be applied via CSS clip-path to identify primary creative action triggers and workspace tab anchors.

## Components

### Buttons & Kinetic Triggers
- **Primary Execution Button**: Solid `#F23827` vermilion fill with `#08090B` bold typography (`Syne` or `JetBrains Mono`). Sharp `0px` radius. Hover shifts to `#FF6A2A` amber with an intense laser shadow: `0 0 16px rgba(242, 56, 39, 0.5)`. Active compression state lowers opacity to 90%.
- **Ghost / Telemetry Button**: Background transparent; `1px solid #2A2A2E` border; text in `#E8E5E1`. Hover initiates a hairline highlight: border transitions to `#F23827`, text glows with vermilion luminescence, and background shifts to `rgba(242, 56, 39, 0.05)`.
- **Destructive / Override**: Outlined in red with crosshatch telemetry indicators on hover.

### Lenses, Genome Badges & Chips
- **Genome Metadata Tag**: Razor-sharp tags with `#171719` background, `1px solid #2A2A2E`, text in `#8F8C87` using `JetBrains Mono` (`lens-tag`).
- **Active Lens Tag**: Vermilion border (`#F23827`) with vermilion text and an ambient `#F23827` corner pip.
- **Format**: Prefixed with telemetry indexes (e.g., `// GEN:09`, `[LENS:NARRATIVE]`).

### Cards & Workstation Panels
- Structural enclosures built on `#171719` with absolute 1px `#2A2A2E` outer boundaries.
- Panels feature a top status bar bearing monospaced telemetry headers, node status indices, and micro-grid indicators.
- Content inside panels relies on internal `space-md` padding and border dividers rather than card-in-card nesting.

### Input Fields & Terminal Prompts
- Background is deep void (`#08090B`) inset within `#171719` surfaces.
- Border is `1px solid #2A2A2E`. On focus, the field features a `1px solid #F23827` glow with a flashing vermilion block cursor.
- Text entry employs `JetBrains Mono` or `Inter`, prefaced with monospaced command tags (e.g., `PROMPT >`).

### Checkboxes, Toggles & Radios
- **Checkboxes**: Pure square frame (`14px x 14px`), `1px solid #2A2A2E`. Checked state fills solid with `#F23827` containing an obsidian inverted micro-tick or center square dot.
- **Switches / Binary Toggles**: Hard-edged mechanical slider track (`#08090B` interior, `1px solid #2A2A2E`). Indicator block moves linearly with instant transitions and vermilion illumination when engaged.

### Workstation Lists & Spec Sheets
- Rows delineated by 1px bottom borders in `#2A2A2E`.
- Hovering over a list item activates a titanium highlight (`#1E1E22`) and reveals kinetic orange arrow indicators (`->`) at the trailing edge.

### Specialized Creative Components
- **Idea Metabolism Nodes**: Multi-state interactive concept frames displaying creative health scores, narrative tension gauges, and Grand Prix viability indices.
- **Telemetry Scrubber**: Frame-accurate visual timeline with glowing vermilion playhead markers, micro-timecodes, and split-second keyframe anchors.
