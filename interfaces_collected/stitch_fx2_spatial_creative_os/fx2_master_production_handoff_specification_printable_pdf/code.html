5. Visual Identity & Master Color Pipeline Rigor

  • Visual System: Obsidian Prism — Deep charcoal/obsidian surfaces (#0d0e10, #121315), razor #38393b structural lines, high-contrast monospace data telemetry, and crimson active indicators (#f23827).
  • Working Color Space: ACEScc AP1 (SMPTE ST 2065-1 / ST 2067-50).
  • Distinct Separation of Stages:
    1. Camera Input Device Transform (IDT): Normalizes camera RAW to ACEScc AP1.
    2. Grading & Look Management (LMT): Creative color timing within AP1 wide gamut.
    3. Output Device Transform (ODT): Kodak 2383 D65 theatrical print emulation.
  • Technicolor 3-Strip Rule: Confined strictly to explicitly tagged subjective-memory/hallucination sequences (PSYCHE_TAG). It is strictly barred from automatically triggering based on Z-depth.
