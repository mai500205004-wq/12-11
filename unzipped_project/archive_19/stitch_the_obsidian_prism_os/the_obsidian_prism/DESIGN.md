## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-end cinematic spatial computing tailored for executive creative directors, film colorists, auteur game designers, and generative artists. The persona is exacting, visionary, and steeped in high-production value.

The design movement merges **Dark Luxury Editorial** with **Spatial Razor-Glassmorphism**:
- **Atmospheric Obsidian Void:** An absolute deep-space obsidian bedrock that eliminates visual noise and allows cinematic media to emanate organic light.
- **Volumetric Edge Light & Optical Refraction:** Translucent dark glass panes defined not by flat outlines, but by razor-thin, light-harvesting anisotropic borders and subtle laser dispersion.
- **Tension of Craft:** A sharp, deliberate juxtaposition between the theatrical prestige of high-contrast display typography and the relentless precision of an engineering HUD.
- **Directorial Poise:** Interactions must feel weighted, instant, and tactile, simulating physical glass prisms lit by internal crimson lasers.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around spatial contrast, void-level absorption, and selective optical radiation:

- **Primary (`#F23827` - Cinematic Laser):** An incandescent, pure spectral red-orange. Used exclusively for focal focus, laser reticles, active timeline playheads, record triggers, and micro-edge highlights.
- **Secondary (`#D4AF37` - Liquid Pale Gold):** High-luxury metallic accent used sparingly for prestigious milestones, master pass indicators, audio ceiling limits, and curation badges.
- **Tertiary (`#2EE9A6` - Spectral Phosphor):** A calibrated green laser emitter reserved for live telemetry, active render stems, real-time node links, and sync health.
- **Neutral Core (`#08090B` - Deep Obsidian):**
  - `Surface Void` (`#08090B`): Absolute zero canvas depth.
  - `Surface Glass Dark` (`rgba(14, 16, 20, 0.72)`): Base spatial container with 24px backdrop-filter blur.
  - `Surface Glass Raised` (`rgba(22, 25, 32, 0.85)`): Elevated panels and floating inspector heads.
  - `Border Prism` (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` top/left grading down to `rgba(242, 56, 39, 0.25)` bottom/right): Anisotropic optical rim light.
  - `Text Core` (`#F8F9FA`): Highest optical legibility text.
  - `Text Dim` (`rgba(248, 249, 250, 0.45)`): Metadata, frame counters, and inactive labels.

## Typography

The system operates on an uncompromising dichotomy:
1. **The Editorial Voice (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for dramatic statements, scene titles, creative acts, and master modal headers. Set with tight line-heights and slight tracking compression to command the spatial frame like a luxury cinema title card.
2. **The Instrument Engine (`Space Mono`):** Every interaction metric, frame rate, LUT value, node tag, and system label is rendered in cold, calibrated monospaced geometry.

**Rules:**
- All `label-*` and operational metrics must be transformed to uppercase with expanded letter-spacing (`0.08em` to `0.12em`) to function as precision instrument readouts.
- Display serifs must never be used for micro-copy or dense technical lists; retain serif solely for macro-scale creative hierarchy.

## Elevation & Depth

Spatial layering relies on glass refraction, dark ambient occlusion, and dynamic laser ray-tracing rather than standard drop shadows:

- **Level 0 (Obsidian Bedrock):** `#08090B` solid matte background with micro radial gradient falloff from the mouse position or active laser beam.
- **Level 1 (Subsurface Glass Panels):** `rgba(14, 16, 20, 0.70)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px) saturate(180%)`. Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Level 2 (Floating Command Drawers & Inspector Trays):** `rgba(20, 23, 30, 0.82)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(32px)`. Shadow: `0 16px 40px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12) inset`.
- **Level 3 (Focused Modals & Spatial Overlays):** `rgba(24, 28, 36, 0.94)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(48px)`. Border receives a multi-stop volumetric gradient (`#F23827` at active corner tapering to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`). Shadow: `0 32px 80px -16px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.95), 0 0 32px -4px rgba(242, 56, 39, 0.2)`.

## Shapes

The geometric form factor is calibrated at **Soft / Razor-Engineered** (`roundedness: 1`):
- Standard interactive elements, panels, and micro-hud chips maintain a precise `0.25rem` (`4px`) radius, mimicking cut sapphire and polished optical lens chassis.
- Large floating viewport canvases and major modal windows utilize `0.5rem` (`8px`) max corner radii to avoid softening the disciplined directorial tone.
- Rounded pills and hyper-organic curves are strictly prohibited; geometry must remain angular, architectural, and razor-sharp.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Laser Master (Primary):** Background `rgba(242, 56, 39, 0.15)`, border `1px solid #F23827`, text `#F8F9FA`. On hover: background `#F23827`, color `#08090B`, box-shadow `0 0 24px rgba(242, 56, 39, 0.5)`. Monospaced, tracked uppercase text.
- **Glass Utility (Secondary):** Background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, text `rgba(248, 249, 250, 0.8)`. Hover introduces a light-sweep reflection and border illumination to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.3)`.
- **Ghost Action:** Zero background, `Space Mono` typography, subtle crosshair icons that turn `#F23827` on focus.

### Chips & Telemetry Tags
- Compact `18px` height containers with `0.25rem` corners.
- Background `rgba(8, 9, 11, 0.8)` with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
- Includes a leading `4px` glowing spectral diode (green `#2EE9A6` for synced, red `#F23827` for active record, gold `#D4AF37` for locked).

### Lists & Keyframe Feeds
- Zebra stripes are disallowed. Rows are divided by hairline rules (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`).
- Hover state: Row background activates to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02)` while a `2px` vertical `#F23827` indicator bar appears pinned to the left edge.
- Data values set in `Space Mono` body levels; item titles set in crisp, unadorned light weights.

### Checkboxes & Toggle Switches
- **Checkboxes:** Square `14px` x `14px` frames, `2px` radius, hairline border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)`. Selected state fills with `#F23827` with a razor-thin black vector tick.
- **Toggle Switches:** Angular `32px` x `16px` sliding track with `2px` corners. Switch slider is a crisp square glass block that shifts from dim gray to incandescent red with a trailing optical glow.

### Input Fields & Parameter Steppers
- Dark inset trenches (`rgba(4, 5, 7, 0.8)`) framed by `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
- Active focus state: Edge transitions to `#F23827` with an ambient glow blur of `6px` at `0.25` opacity; label flashes monospaced red tracking.
- Numeric parameters include interactive scrub handles styled as technical tick marks.

### Cards & Spatial Nodes
- Translucent prism slabs backed by `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`.
- Anisotropic perimeter highlight simulating edge illumination.
- Header features high-contrast `Playfair Display` titles framed next to monospaced timecodes or node parameters (`Space Mono`).

### Creative OS Specialized Components
- **Cinematic Timeline Scrubber:** Obsidian track with sub-pixel tick marks, laser-red playhead (`#F23827`) emitting a vertical refraction beam spanning the whole canvas.
- **Node Matrix Connectors:** Curved optical light pipes in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)` pulsing to `#2EE9A6` or `#F23827` during dynamic data transfer.
- **Vector Reticle Cursor:** Dynamic SVG reticle displaying current mouse spatial X/Y coordinates in `label-sm` beside the cursor arrow.
