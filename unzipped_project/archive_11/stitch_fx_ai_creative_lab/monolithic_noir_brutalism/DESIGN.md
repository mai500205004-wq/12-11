## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a focused, hyper-tactile creative operating environment for film directors, writers' rooms, and lead brand strategists. It is built on the convergence of Monolithic Swiss Brutalism and Kinetic Noir: uncompromising structural discipline, asymmetric high-density data matrices, and stark, gallery-grade contrast. 

The aesthetic rejects generic SaaS archetypes—no multi-stop neon blue gradients, no inflated pill shapes, and no decorative card elevation. Instead, the UI reads as an elite command deck: razor-sharp structural frameworks, technical telemetry readouts, cinematic screenplay rulers, and precise tactile triggers. Every element suggests authorial control, structural permanence, and raw computational horsepower.

## Colors

The color architecture is built around three calibrated layers:

- **Obsidian Substrates:** The background canvas relies on deep black `#070709`, framed by working panel surfaces at `#0C0D11` and active interactive surfaces at `#13141B`. These dark values prevent eye fatigue during prolonged writers' room sessions while establishing stark tonal contrast.
- **Bone-White Typographic Tiers:** High-impact headings and critical readouts use bone-white `#F5F5F2`. Secondary metadata, script lines, and structural notes use muted zinc `#DDDCD5` and `#A1A1AA`. Deep background context text rests at `#71717A`.
- **Kinetic Telemetry Signals:** Functional highlights are deployed sparingly. Electric Vermilion (`#FF4800`) signifies real-time synthesis, destructive actions, focus locks, and engine execution states. Optical Phosphor Lime (`#22C55E`) denotes verified branch histories, completed renders, and telemetry health. Surfaces remain strictly monochrome until live creative telemetry triggers a signal state.

## Typography

The type hierarchy balances Swiss modernist architecture with cold computational precision:

- **Primary Display & Headlines (Space Grotesk):** Tight tracking, mechanical geometric aperatures, and authoritative stroke profiles define workspace chapters, divergent scenario banners, and creative brief headers.
- **Editorial & Screenplay Body (IBM Plex Sans):** Uncompromising structural clarity across long-form prompt chains, script beats, and strategic manifests. Built-in bi-directional support gracefully harmonizes with IBM Plex Sans Arabic for LTR/RTL workflows without horizontal reflow distortion.
- **Instrument Readouts & Script Timestamps (JetBrains Mono):** Standardized across model parameter dials, lateral branch tags, prompt token counters, and screenplay margin notations. Always displayed in clean uppercase or tabular figures for dense numeric readouts.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow an asymmetric 12-column Swiss grid constructed with monolithic panel boundaries. Every axis emphasizes density, structural alignment, and clear visual order:

- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column matrix with `gutter-desktop: 1.5rem` and outer perimeter `margin-desktop: 2.5rem`. Modules form unified command walls with continuous, co-dependent structural dividers rather than floating islands.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 6-column reflow where telemetry drawers collapse into tactical edge switches and canvas panels span full horizontal widths.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column stack utilizing minimum margins (`margin: 1rem`) and unified `1rem` gutters. Monolithic blocks convert into horizontal scrolling tape strips or modular stacked accordion shelves.
- **Rhythm & Alignments:** Gaps strictly conform to a 4px/8px structural baseline. Margins inside modules hug internal borders closely (`space-md`), preserving screen estate for multi-track prompt generation and parallel storyboard sequences.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids blurred drop shadows and elevated floating cards. Depth is established through monolithic tonal stratification and crisp mechanical borders:

- **Zero Diffusion:** Shadows are disabled across standard operational surfaces (`box-shadow: none`). No soft ambient blurs or elevated z-axis diffusion.
- **Boundary Precision:** Structural differentiation is achieved via hard `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)` framing lines. Active focal zones transition crisply to `rgba(255, 72, 0, 0.7)` or `#F5F5F2`.
- **Tonal Stepping:** Surfaces layer according to semantic depth: Canvas Floor (`#070709`) → Structural Containers (`#0C0D11`) → Interactive Terminals (`#13141B`) → Hover & Active Overlays (`#1E202B`).
- **Tactical Cutouts & Overlays:** Modal dialogues, contextual command palettes, and drawer components mount flush onto the screen with a sharp `1px` high-contrast outer border and a pure obsidian backdrop overlay (`rgba(7, 7, 9, 0.88)` with zero backdrop blur).

## Shapes

The geometric framework is unyielding: **0px border radius across all components**.

- Every surface, modal, trigger button, input cell, and telemetry badge terminates in sharp 90-degree right angles.
- Corner geometry occasionally integrates 45-degree chamfers (cut corners of 4px to 8px) on modal dialog anchors and signal badges to underscore its technical switchboard heritage.
- Eliminating softened radii reinforces the structural permanence of an architectural drafting board or high-end soundstage console.

## Components

### Buttons & Switches
- **Primary Action (Execute/Generate):** Solid Electric Vermilion (`#FF4800`) fill with deep Obsidian (`#070709`) bold uppercase text (`label-caps`). Sharp corners. Active state: `#FF5500` with high-frequency micro-scale inversion.
- **Secondary / Tactical:** Transparent core, 1px perimeter border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`), bone-white text (`#DDDCD5`). Hover shifts background to `#13141B` and border to `#F5F5F2`.
- **Switchboard Toggles:** Monospaced two-state switches (`[ON]` / `[OFF]`) rendered in tabular brackets. Activating flips state to Phosphor Lime (`#22C55E`) text with instantaneous hard transitions (0ms ease).

### Input Fields & Prompt Consoles
- **Terminal Input:** Zero outer ambient border; top and bottom bounded by 1px technical lines. JetBrains Mono font (`body-script`). 
- **Focus State:** Active cursor displays a solid vermilion block (`▋`). Input background shifts from `#0C0D11` to `#13141B`.
- **Telemetry Counter:** Bottom-right corner embedded with monospace tokens/context metrics (`JetBrains Mono`, `10px`, `#71717A`).

### Cards & Narrative Nodes
- **Monolithic Container:** 1px perimeter border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`), flat `#0C0D11` fill. Header regions separated by a hairline 1px horizontal shelf containing small-caps metadata (`label-caps`).
- **Lateral Branch Node:** A split-screen card with vertical 1px divider lines indicating divergent creative paths. Highlighted branches carry a 2px solid Vermilion left-accent border.

### Chips & Telemetry Tags
- **Data Tags:** Raw square badges with no border radius. Solid `#13141B` backing, 1px border (`rgba(255,255,255,0.12)`), uppercase monospaced text (`mono-telemetry`). Status indicators use 6px solid square glyphs (`■`) in Lime (`#22C55E`) or Vermilion (`#FF4800`).

### Checkboxes & Selection Controls
- **Check Matrix:** Square 14px × 14px box, 1px border. Checked state renders a solid vermilion square fill with an inner black center dot. No rounded corners, no smooth checkmark animations.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Screenplay Telemetry Ruler:** Fixed vertical strip with monospace line numbering, scene slug indicators (`EXT. / INT.`), and millisecond-accurate script pacing marks.
- **Divergence Engine Dial:** Horizontal linear scale tracking model temperature and lateral deviation scores, marked with notched 1px technical ticks and live numeric readouts.
