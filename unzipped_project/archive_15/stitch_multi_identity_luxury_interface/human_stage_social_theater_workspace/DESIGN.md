## Brand & Style

This design system translates the communal vitality, oral tradition, and documentary intensity of Middle Eastern street-level theater into a digital workspace for creative directors, playwrights, and cultural documentarians. The environment prioritizes human presence, visceral dialogue, and dramaturgical rhythm over sterile SaaS mechanics.

### Emotional Landscape & Persona
- **Documentary Authenticity:** Evokes 35mm film stills, street recordings, sun-baked sandstone courtyards, script annotations in margins, and the hush of a rehearsal room before dusk.
- **Communal Gravity:** Eliminates hyper-individualist metrics in favor of ensemble states, communal roundtables (the "Majlis" or circle), and social polyphony.
- **Theatrical Chiaroscuro:** Uses warm, directional stage lighting against charcoal shadows to pull focal elements forward while allowing background context to rest in deep twilight.

### Design Movement: Warm Editorial Naturalism & Theatrical Brutalism
The interface blends high-contrast Middle Eastern editorial typography with tactile, grounded architectural surfaces. It rejects cold grays, corporate blue tints, and clinical glassmorphism. Surfaces emulate pressed earth, raw umber textiles, and stage wood lit by warm tungsten and golden hour sun.

## Colors

The system is calibrated for a rich dark mode steeped in earth and stagecraft tones. Every hue is warmed with iron oxides and amber undertones rather than neutral grays.

### Core Roles & Material Swatches
- **Primary (`#d9653b` - Desert Dusk Terracotta):** Represents the focal point, active dramaturgical markers, main stage cues, and primary interactive affordances.
- **Secondary (`#f39c12` - Golden Amber):** The flicker of tungsten lamps, urgent director revisions, active speaker indicators, and critical annotations.
- **Tertiary (`#8c4327` - Burnt Sienna / Raw Umber):** Subdued interactive states, script chapter dividing lines, and secondary badge fills.
- **Neutral Foreground (`#ede6de` - Raw Linen & Desert Sand):** The primary reading text, dialogue transcripts, and tactile typography. Never pure white (`#ffffff`).
- **Base Canvas (`#141110` - Deep Raw Charcoal):** The root theater floor, night exterior, and void surrounding the stage.
- **Layered Surfaces (`#1c1715` / `#26201d` - Rammed Earth Layers):** Script card backdrops, elevated workbenches, communal drawer layers, and sideboards.
- **Muted Text & Border Inks (`#a89f91` & `#382e29`):** Metadata, stage direction italics, and subtle tectonic partition lines.

## Typography

The typographic system creates an interplay between classical dramatic authority and fluid human dialogue:
- **Noto Serif** anchors titles, character monologues, act headers, and documentary quotes. It conveys the weight of Levant and North African printing traditions, literary revivals, and dramaturgical script folios.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** delivers humanist, readable, and warm UI elements, stage notes, field metadata, and functional controls without cold geometric sterility.
- **Stage Directions & Vox-Populi Transcripts:** Set in italicized `body-lg` or `body-md` with relaxed line spacing (`1.7x`) to encourage sustained reading during real-time rehearsals.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial model relies on a responsive 12-column theatrical stage grid on desktop (reflowing to 6 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile), referencing both architectural arcades and the spatial blocking of actors.

### Spatial Rhythms
- **Canvas Margins:** Generous outer margins framing the focal work like a proscenium arch. Content breathes inside a warm, dark perimeter.
- **The Dramaturgical Pane:** Split-screen asymmetric compositions pairing longform documentary narratives (7 columns) against director prompt decks, character lists, and audio soundscapes (5 columns).
- **Ensemble Circle Breakouts:** Circular or rounded gallery configurations for character arcs and chorus voices, maintaining `space-md` between actor nodes and `space-xl` between scene milestones.
- **Mobile Reflow:** Single-column stacked rehearsal stream. Margins tighten to `margin-mobile` to preserve dialogue width, while floating stage controls dock to the bottom thumb zone.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is treated as stage depth and warm tungsten cast, completely eschewing cold gray box-shadows and plastic specular highlights.

### Elevation Hierarchy
1. **Stage Floor (Level 0 - `#141110`):** The foundational plane. Monolithic, grounded, absorbing light.
2. **Platform & Rehearsal Boards (Level 1 - `#1c1715`):** Bordered with a delicate mineral hairline border (`rgba(237, 230, 222, 0.08)`). No elevation shadow needed; hierarchy is achieved through tone shifts.
3. **Dialogue Cards & Director Slates (Level 2 - `#26201d`):** Elevated by an ambient, low-angle ember glow: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(217, 101, 59, 0.08), 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.
4. **Active Monologues, Modals & Floating Tools (Level 3 - `#2e2622`):** Direct theatrical highlight. Rimmed with an amber-tinted top edge (`1px solid rgba(243, 156, 18, 0.25)`) and backed by a deep twilight drop shadow: `0 16px 48px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85), 0 0 24px -2px rgba(217, 101, 59, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts soft, architectural forms reminiscent of hand-carved limestone and pressed terracotta tiles. 

- **Level 1 (Soft):** Default containers, cue cards, dialogue snippets, and input fields utilize `0.25rem` (4px) corner radii. This maintains structural rigor and textual dignity without harsh, knife-edge brutalism.
- **Ensemble Nodes & Actor Avatars:** Distinctly round (`50%` pill/circle) to reinforce the communal concept of the circle, the chorus, and human faces in the crowd.
- **Stamps & Action Tags:** Squared with microscopic chams (`2px`) emulating printed script marks, clapper slates, and archival tape.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary ("Stage Cue"):** Filled with Desert Dusk Terracotta (`#d9653b`), text in `#ede6de` with uppercase `label-md` tracking. Hover initiates a warm tungsten luminosity (`background: #e2764e; box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(217, 101, 59, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary ("Director Note"):** Transparent background with a hand-cast mineral border (`1px solid rgba(237, 230, 222, 0.2)`), resting in `#ede6de`. On hover, border warms to `#f39c12` with text shifting into `#f39c12`.
- **Communal / Destructive ("Drop Scene"):** Dark background `#26201d` with muted ochre-red text (`#cf5a32`). No bright synthetic reds.

### Dialogue & Scene Cards
- Background set to Rammed Earth Layer (`#1c1715`) with a hairline border (`#382e29`).
- Character header uses `Noto Serif` (`title-md`) in `#f39c12`.
- Spoken lines set in `Plus Jakarta Sans` (`body-lg`) in `#ede6de`.
- Left border features a 3px accent pipe indicating the active scene's emotional tempo (terracotta for high tension, warm sand for exposition, amber for chorus dialogue).

### Chips & Character Tags
- Pill forms styled with `rgba(217, 101, 59, 0.12)` background, bounded by `1px solid rgba(217, 101, 59, 0.3)`. Text in `label-sm` sand `#ede6de`.
- Chorus and ensemble tags utilize subtle umber tinting with golden amber dot markers indicating active line presence.

### Inputs & Script Prompts
- Solid base of `#191413` with a recessed top border.
- Floating placeholder labels styled in `Plus Jakarta Sans` muted sand (`#a89f91`).
- Focus state casts an amber outline (`1px solid #f39c12`) with a subtle ambient wash, reflecting light back onto the workspace canvas.

### Lists & Social Vox-Populi Feeds
- Continuous narrative stream dividers use faint terracotta dust lines (`rgba(217, 101, 59, 0.15)`).
- Left-hand gutters display documentary timecodes and street recording geo-tags in monospaced, small-caps label text.
- Interactive list items elevate smoothly to `#221c19` on hover with a warm amber chevron reveal.

### Checkboxes & Stage Markers
- Custom square markers with softened corners (`2px`).
- Inactive state: `#1c1715` with `#382e29` border.
- Active state: Fills with `#d9653b`, presenting a sand-colored check or scene glyph, signifying script lock or actor read-through completion.
