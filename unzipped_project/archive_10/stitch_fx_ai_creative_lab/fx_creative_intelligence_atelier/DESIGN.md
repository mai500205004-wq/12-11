## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an Arabic-first creative atelier environment for executive creative directors, narrative architects, and cinematic visionaries. The aesthetic fuses haute-couture editorial print with 70mm widescreen cinema and disciplined intelligence interfaces. 

### Visual Tenets
- **Cinematic Chiaroscuro:** Deep obsidian shadows punctuated by champagne brass and incandescent bone accents. The screen is approached not as a workspace panel, but as a light-sealed projection room and tactile editorial workbench.
- **Editorial Typography as Architecture:** Arabic calligraphy traditions meet modernist structural discipline. Text is treated as sculptural mass, calibrated for authoritative right-to-left optical balance.
- **Analogue Direction & Provenance:** Widescreen apertures (2.39:1 aspect ratio cues), subtle frame timecodes, metadata margins, and physical film slate cues replace generic SaaS dashboard metaphors.
- **Deliberate Restraint:** Zero saturated blues, zero playful candy buttons, and zero bubbly pills. Every visual unit carries the gravity of an archival folio or cinematic master negative.

## Colors

The palette operates in strict tonal layers of carbon, graphite, brushed brass, and museum bone.

### Palette Hierarchy
- **Canvas Obsidian (`#07080A`):** The absolute foundational background. Non-reflective, infinite negative space.
- **Surface Graphite (`#0E1015`):** Secondary container surface for storyboards, dials, and workspace modules.
- **Elevated Charcoal (`#141720`):** Floating inspector panels, flyouts, and aperture frames.
- **Champagne Brass (`#E5C483` & `#D4AF37`):** Primary focal accents, active dialectical levers, run markers, and key status indicators.
- **Burnished Bronze (`#C6A052`):** Hairline borders, structural grid dividers, and inactive calibration nodes.
- **Editorial Bone (`#F8F7F4`):** Primary headline and Arabic display text.
- **Warm Parchment (`#E2DFD7`):** Body narrative copy and descriptive prompt scripts.
- **Muted Stone (`#9B988E`):** Micro-metadata, technical timestamps, and filmic aspect notations.
- **Tension Ember (`#E0533C`):** Subdued cinnabar indicator reserved strictly for divergent states, critical anomalies, and prompt stress-testing.

## Typography

The typographical landscape is architected with strict Arabic-first optical balance. Display and narrative titles invoke the structural majesty of traditional classical script coupled with contemporary editorial serifs, supported by structural grotesques for working copy and monospaced technical timecodes.

### Hierarchy & Script Pairing
- **Arabic Display & Narrative:** Set with rich ascenders, generous baseline leading, and natural ligatures to preserve script cadence without vertical crampedness.
- **Latin Display & Editorial Intertitles:** Rendered in high-contrast editorial serifs (`Noto Serif`) for dramatic chapter headers, director credits, and concept aphorisms.
- **Body & Operational Copy (`IBM Plex Sans`):** Selected for its clear Arabic counterpart (`IBM Plex Sans Arabic`), delivering neutral, razor-sharp legibility at dense scales across multi-column scripts.
- **Technical Annotations (`Space Mono`):** Applied strictly to frame markers, aspect rations (`2.39:1`), seed parameters, creative stimulus values, and timecode readouts (`00:14:32:08`).

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts are inspired by cinema frame composition and luxury broadsheet publishing. Rather than dense, uniform app tiles, the interface deploys asymmetrical master-and-detail alignments with generous negative space.

### Structural Composition
- **Grid Structure:** 12-column asymmetric grid on desktop allowing wide cinematic central preview zones flanked by surgical editorial sidebars (e.g., 3-column instrument bar RTL right, 9-column narrative viewport left).
- **RTL Fluidity:** Natural flow originates from the right margin. Primary conceptual navigation and provocation dials anchor to the visual start (right), directing creative focus toward the output canvas (left).
- **Aspect Framing:** Storyboard canvases and media output regions enforce rigid filmic proportions: `2.39:1` (Anamorphic Cinema), `1.85:1` (Flat Standard), or `1.33:1` (Academy Ratio), bracketed with archival bounding marks.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this system is created through physical light containment and material layering rather than generic drop shadows.

### Dimensional Stratification
- **Hairline Framing:** Depth is demarcated by 1px or 0.5px brass-infused borders (`rgba(229, 196, 131, 0.14)` resting; `rgba(229, 196, 131, 0.45)` active).
- **Photographic Occlusion:** Overlays and context drawers use deep glass containment with heavy blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`) backed by 90% opaque obsidian (`#07080A`), creating the illusion of viewing through polarized, anti-reflective optical glass.
- **Ambient Specular Sheen:** Active frames project an ultra-diffused, warm champagne halo (`box-shadow: 0 0 35px -8px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08)`).
- **Tension States:** Divergent stimulus panels project an ominous ember bleed (`box-shadow: 0 0 30px -10px rgba(224, 83, 60, 0.12)`).

## Shapes

The geometry is unapologetically sharp (`0px` roundedness). Corners are crisp, linear, and architectural, mirroring museum picture frames, slate clappers, and physical director viewfinders. 

Where micro-delimiters exist (such as state chips or aperture tags), chamfered 45-degree micro-notches (2px) or pure rectangles are used. Pill shapes and rounded buttons are explicitly excluded to maintain an avant-garde editorial tone.

## Components

### Buttons & Trigger Prisms
- **Primary Action (Directorial Run):** Rectangular monolithic block. Background in Champagne Brass (`#E5C483`), text set in absolute Canvas Obsidian (`#07080A`), bold typographical tracking. Zero border-radius. Subtle hover transitions to a high-polish bone gold (`#F5DEB3`).
- **Ghost Action (Aperture Selector):** Transparent core with a razor-thin 1px border of Burnished Bronze (`rgba(198, 160, 82, 0.3)`). Text in Editorial Bone (`#F8F7F4`). Hover shifts border to 100% Brass with an interior subtle graphite fill.
- **Tension Trigger (Constraint Reversal):** Inverted outline in Ember (`#E0533C`) with monospace micro-indexing.

### Inputs & Prompt Canvases
- **Prompt Composer:** Full-bleed editorial script slate. Dark Surface Graphite (`#0E1015`) with a hairline top and bottom brass separator. Arabic text enters large (`20px`), supported by a persistent bottom metadata ribbon showing token velocity, temperature, and semantic density in monospaced bone.
- **Numeric Calibration Inputs:** Monospaced Latin numerals set alongside right-aligned Arabic semantic descriptors. Adjustments operate via horizontal drag scrubbers calibrated like analogue lens rings.

### Dial & Thinking Instruments
Bespoke tactile instrumentation for the five generative creative axes:
1. **الاستفزاز (Provocation):** Vertical stepped sliding scale with brass needle indicators.
2. **المقاربة والتناظر (Analogies):** Dual-node crosshair balance gauge.
3. **المحفز العشوائي (Random Stimulus):** Stepped mechanical tumbler with hairline divisions.
4. **التفكير المعاكس (Opposite Thinking):** Inverted dark/light toggling prism.
5. **كسر القيود (Constraint Reversal):** Angular tension lever highlighting in crimson ember when active.

### Storyboard Cards & Framing Apertures
- Rigid rectangular frames bound by 0.5px hairline brass borders.
- Top metadata strip displays shot index (`SCENE 04 / TAKE 02`) in monospaced stone text (`#9B988E`).
- Interior letterboxing conforms to cinematic widescreen (`2.39:1`).
- Active card inspection draws four architectural corner ticks (`+`) in pure champagne gold at each vertex.

### Selection Controls & Chips
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Sharp square indicators (14px × 14px) containing an interior solid brass diamond or square upon selection.
- **Aperture Chips:** Compact rectangular tags with hairline stone borders, displaying film stocks, model checkpoints, and director modes in monospaced caps.
