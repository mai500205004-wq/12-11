## Brand & Style

This design system expresses absolute sovereignty, forbidden palatial prestige, and dark theatrical majesty. Drawing aesthetic DNA from imperial opera houses, grand dynastic throne halls, obsidian monoliths, and heavy crimson velvet drapery, the visual language commands reverence and deliberate restraint.

The style operates as an orchestration of **Dark Luxury Architectural Precision**:
- **Sovereign Gravity:** Monumental, grounded dark obsidian foundations balanced by piercing, razor-sharp hairlines.
- **Cinematic Theatrics:** Selective, highly intentional illumination reminiscent of focused stage lights grazing deep velvet textures.
- **Imperial Discipline:** Strict proportional alignments, ceremonial whitespace, and zero casual softness. Shapes are severe, stately, and monolithic.
- **Atmospheric Power:** Deep tonal gradation from near-void obsidian into rich black cherry, sliced by luminous ruby edges.

## Colors

The palette establishes an uncompromising sovereign hierarchy. The canvas does not use flat digital grays; every dark tone is steeped in obsidian stone and cold charcoal undertones, creating a resonant backdrop for imperial crimson.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Sovereign (`#D91424`):** Imperial Crimson. Used for high-impact actions, authoritative seals, active states, and focal moments.
- **Secondary Luminescence (`#FF2E43`):** Radiant Ruby. Applied selectively to hairline rims, focus rings, and high-energy hover pulses.
- **Tertiary Depths (`#8B0000`):** Sanguine Ochre / Dried Blood. Serves as dark crimson fill states, tonal backdrops, and gradient terminators.
- **Deepest Void (`#080708`):** Absolute Onyx base. The deepest canvas floor.
- **Structural Obsidian (`#120F11`, `#1A1518`):** Elevated container surfaces, echoing cut black marble and polished tourmaline.
- **Text & Crests:** Platinum Ivory (`#FDFBF7`) for display typography and primary copy; Burnished Ash (`#A39B98`) for secondary body text; Muted Obsidian Stone (`#5A5255`) for disabled or structural dividers.

## Typography

The typography couples the theatrical, high-fashion authority of high-contrast serif letterforms with the clean, architectural precision of a geometric sans.

- **Headlines (Bodoni Moda):** Radiates imperial lineage, dramatic editorial contrast, and royal prestige. Display titles command space and are rendered with subtle negative tracking for monumental presence.
- **Body & Functional Hierarchy (Outfit):** Provides a crisp, ultra-legible, geometric counterbalance to the serif's ornate presence. Set at lighter weights (`300`) for body copy to evoke airy refinement, and bolded with expanded tracking (`0.15em` to `0.2em`) for ceremonial metadata, uppercase labels, and badges.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts are treated as ceremonial halls: measured, balanced, and deliberately paced. Whitespace is architectural negative space, allowing high-contrast typography and crimson highlights to command the field.

### Grid & Composition
- **Desktop (1440px+):** 12-column symmetrical fluid grid with wide margins (`3rem`) and generous gutters (`1.5rem`). Max-width containers default to `1280px` or `1440px` to maintain palatial proportions without horizontal overstretch.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with 2rem margins and 1.25rem gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with tight edge margins (`1.25rem`) and 1rem gutters, shifting multi-column monoliths into vertically stacked architectural slabs.

### Rhythmic Discipline
Components employ generous internal breathing room (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) combined with micro-spaced internal grouping (`space-xs` and `space-sm`). Dense, cluttered clusters are prohibited.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth does not rely on generic drop shadows. Instead, it is expressed through obsidian layering, dark crimson atmospheric backdrops, and 1px ruby hairline rim lighting.

### Depth Archetypes
1. **The Foundation Surface (Level 0):** Canvas base `#080708` with an optional radial gradient vignette that fades outward into pure void.
2. **Elevated Monoliths (Level 1):** Solid `#110E10` or translucent obsidian glass (`rgba(17, 14, 16, 0.75)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`). Outlined by a crisp `1px` border of `rgba(217, 20, 36, 0.22)`.
3. **Ascended Chambers (Level 2):** Layered cards, dialogs, and floating menus in `#191417`. Enclosed in `1px` borders of `rgba(255, 46, 67, 0.45)`, accompanied by a diffuse outer crimson halo: `0 12px 40px -10px rgba(217, 20, 36, 0.25), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(255, 46, 67, 0.3)`.
4. **Specular Rim Lighting:** Interactive cards and active elements project a top-edge linear highlight gradient (`transparent -> rgba(255, 46, 67, 0.6) -> transparent`) simulating overhead stage light catching the edge of polished obsidian stone.

## Shapes

The shape system is strictly **Sharp (0)**. 

Curved, pill-shaped, or soft consumer radii are forbidden. Every card, button, modal, tab, and surface terminates in razor-sharp 90-degree corners, evoking precision-cut black marble and imperial stone steles. Subtle 45-degree chamfered cuts (2px to 4px) are permitted exclusively on ceremonial badges and primary buttons to evoke regal signet seals and imperial masonry.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Imperial Sovereign):** Sharp edges, background in deep crimson `#D91424` transitioning to `#B30F1C` on hover. Platinum ivory text (`#FDFBF7`), uppercase label font with `letter-spacing: 0.15em`. Outlined with a top 1px hairline in `#FF2E43`. On active press, the surface deepens to `#8B0000`.
- **Secondary (Obsidian Slab):** Solid `#120E10` fill with a continuous razor 1px border in `rgba(217, 20, 36, 0.35)`. Hovering intensifies the border to full `#FF2E43` and casts a subtle 12px diffuse red ambient aura.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** No fill, transparent background, `#E0DAD5` typography. Hover initiates a crimson underline hairline that expands from the center outward.

### Cards & Panels
- Constructed from `#120E10` stone surfaces.
- Framed by 1px borders of `rgba(217, 20, 36, 0.2)`.
- Hovering an interactive card transitions the border to `rgba(255, 46, 67, 0.5)` and introduces a soft crimson gradient wash over the header area (`linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(139, 0, 0, 0.15) 0%, transparent 100%)`).

### Input Fields
- Monolithic rectangles with a deep obsidian floor (`#0B090A`).
- Bottom border or full 1px border in `rgba(224, 218, 213, 0.15)`.
- Upon focus, the border snaps to `#FF2E43`, backed by a 4px soft crimson glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 12px rgba(217, 20, 36, 0.3)`). Placeholder text is set in muted stone `#5A5255`.

### Chips & Badges
- Sharp, rectangular containers with micro padding (`space-xs` vertical, `space-sm` horizontal).
- Uppercase `label-sm` typography with wide tracking (`0.2em`).
- Surface in `rgba(139, 0, 0, 0.25)` enclosed by a 1px border of `rgba(255, 46, 67, 0.4)`. Text in `#FDFBF7`.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Sharp, square geometries for both checkboxes and selection indicators.
- Inactive: 1px border in `rgba(224, 218, 213, 0.25)`, obsidian background.
- Active/Checked: Filled with Imperial Crimson `#D91424` featuring a central geometric Platinum Ivory square pip or sharp glyph.

### Lists & Tables
- Minimalist stone rows separated strictly by 1px hairlines of `rgba(217, 20, 36, 0.12)`.
- Row hover states generate a subtle dark crimson backdrop wash (`rgba(139, 0, 0, 0.1)`). Table headers employ `label-lg` uppercase typography.

### Specialized Component: The Imperial Seal / Divider
- Section dividers consist of a razor 1px crimson hairline (`rgba(217, 20, 36, 0.3)`) punctuated in the exact center by a diamond emblem (a rotated 6px square in `#FF2E43`) to punctuate transitions between ceremonial views.
